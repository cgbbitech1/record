How it works: the platter has inertia and takes about a second to reach speed. Drag the tonearm by the headshell to any point on the record (dragging lifts the stylus), then press CUE to lower it. Time is set by where the stylus lands: grooves spiral inward at a constant pitch, so the outer edge is the start of side A and each silent band is a gap between tracks. Playback speed comes from the real platter speed, so the wrong RPM (try a 45 at 33⅓) or the pitch slider changes tempo and key, and stopping the motor with the needle down winds the music down. At the end the stylus drops into the locked groove; with auto-return on, the arm lifts, returns and the motor stops. Keys: Space cue, S start/stop.
